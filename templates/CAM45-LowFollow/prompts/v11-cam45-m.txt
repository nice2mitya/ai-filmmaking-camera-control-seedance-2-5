The red-and-black striped capsule in @video1 corresponds to Alex, walking down the pavement at a slow walk, centred in the frame at full length, seen from behind; he turns his head once to look back over his shoulder, showing only a quarter profile of cheek and beard. 【Camera and Event Script】
Shot 1, the only shot: the camera is on a Steadicam in low mode with a 24 mm lens feel, about three metres behind Alex and about 0.85 metres below his eye line, following him as he walks away from the lens at a slow walking pace down the middle of the pavement. It frames him at full length from behind, centred, the street and its lamps stretching ahead of him, and glides smoothly with him for the whole shot, never getting closer or further away.
Alex's performance: He walks wearily with his shoulders low and the rucksack swinging, his mind on nothing. Then a car engine idles up behind him and slows to his walking pace, the car stays out of sight behind him, and only its headlight glow stretches his shadow ahead along the wet pavement, the street lamps unchanged. His shoulders tighten, his stride shortens and his hand closes on the rucksack strap. He glances back once over his shoulder, then faces forward and walks on at the same pace with tighter steps, never looking round again. He says nothing.
Every action is performed with natural, realistic human motion: real weight and balance, momentum carried through each movement, and loose, believable arms and shoulders. The written description of the action decides how it looks. Alex is a real human being with natural skin, hair and clothing; no mannequin, clay figure, capsule or doll appears anywhere, at any moment.
The sound is only his footsteps on wet pavement, a car engine idling behind him at walking pace, the buzz of the streetlights and his quickening breath. There are no words. There is no music of any kind.
The final visuals are live-action and photographic: photoreal cinema shot on an ARRI Alexa 35 with anamorphic lenses, natural film grain, soft diffusion and halation on the lights, shallow depth of field, motivated light and real atmospheric haze.
