The suitcase grows steadily in the frame, the bench and background falling into soft blur, and the move eases out and settles into a held insert: a low, ground-level, three-quarter view of the suitcase, which fills the right-centre of the frame from the paving almost to the top edge, the handle just under the top of the frame, a paving seam running beneath it, the wall and trees behind reduced to soft grey shapes.
