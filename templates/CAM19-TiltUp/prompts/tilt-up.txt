【Camera and Event Script】
One continuous shot with no cuts, on a 24 mm anamorphic lens from a fixed low position about two metres in front of him, almost at floor level, 7 seconds. The camera does not move; only its angle changes.
It opens looking at his black boots on the worn concrete step with the steel escalator edges converging behind them. From the first frame the camera tilts up smoothly and steadily over 4 seconds: boots, then knees and coat hem at about 1.2 seconds, then chest and scarf at about 2.5 seconds, then his face and eyes at about 4 seconds, with the ceiling lights and the long dark escalator running away above and behind him. It then holds a steady frame on his face for the last 3 seconds.
Alex: he stands motionless apart from his breathing. Around 4.5 seconds his eyes lift, as if following something far above, then drop slowly to the lens and hold there, steady and a little afraid. A single slow blink at about 6 seconds. The steel handrails move continuously beside him, and a thin layer of cold haze hangs in the air.
Every action is performed with natural, realistic human motion: real weight and balance, momentum carried through each movement, and loose, believable arms and shoulders. The written description of the action decides how it looks.
Alex is a real human being with natural skin, hair and clothing. No mannequin, clay figure, capsule, faceless figure or doll appears anywhere, at any moment.
Alex keeps his mouth naturally closed and says nothing; there is no narration, and no subtitles or on-screen text appear.
The sound includes only the low hum of the escalators, a fluorescent tube buzzing, a faint train rumble from far below, soft echo and his slow breathing. There is no music of any kind: no score, no song, no singing and no humming, only these natural sounds.
The final visuals are live-action and photographic: a real underground station: twin escalators flanking central concrete stairs, deep cyan fluorescent light, dark blue brick, brushed steel, small worn yellow safety markings, polished wet-looking floor and cold haze. The picture is photoreal live-action cinema: shot on an ARRI Alexa 35 with vintage anamorphic prime lenses, 2.39:1, natural 35mm film grain, a soft 1/4 Black Pro-Mist diffusion on the lights, gentle halation round every bright light source, shallow depth of field with oval anamorphic bokeh, real atmospheric haze and depth, motivated practical light with soft falloff, rich but natural contrast with colour kept in the shadows, and real textured materials everywhere. It must look like a still pulled from a feature film, never like a render, a game or a video effect.
