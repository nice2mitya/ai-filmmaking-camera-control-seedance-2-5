【Subjects and Relationships】
The red-and-black striped capsule in @video1 corresponds to Alex, standing at the foot of the stairs in the middle of the frame, facing the lens, seen first from the boots up. He wears a clean dark jacket over a grey shirt, work boots with cement dust on the toes, a small white cake box held in both hands. The place: A deep underground station: twin escalators flanking wide concrete stairs, deep cyan fluorescent light, dark blue brick walls, brushed steel rails and small worn yellow safety markings on the floor, a cold clean hush. Soft bloom on the tube lights, cyan shadows with a little navy, fine film grain.

【Camera and Event Script】
Shot 1, the only shot: the camera is fixed low on the floor about two metres from Alex with a 24 mm lens feel, looking up at him. It opens on his boots and legs on the pale floor, then tilts slowly up over about four seconds, past his legs and chest, to his face, and holds on his head and shoulders in the middle of the frame for the last three seconds, where his eyes lift and the smile opens, the dark ceiling with its round lights and the concrete stairs behind him. Only the angle changes; the camera never leaves its spot.
Alex's performance: He has been waiting for twenty minutes, rehearsing a smile. He shifts his weight and straightens the cake box in his hands, eyes down. Then something in the distance catches his eye and his whole face opens: his breath stops, his eyebrows lift, his mouth falls open and spreads into a wide smile, his eyes shining, and a short, happy laugh escapes him. He says nothing. No one else appears; his sister is never seen.
Every action is performed with natural, realistic human motion: real weight and balance, momentum carried through each movement, and loose, believable arms and shoulders. The written description of the action decides how it looks. Alex is a real human being with natural skin, hair and clothing; no mannequin, clay figure, capsule or doll appears anywhere, at any moment.
The sound is only the low hum of the escalators, a train arriving far off, a faint echo in the tunnel and his breathing. There are no words. There is no music of any kind.
The final visuals are live-action and photographic: photoreal cinema shot on an ARRI Alexa 35 with anamorphic lenses, natural film grain, soft diffusion and halation on the lights, shallow depth of field, motivated light and real atmospheric haze.
