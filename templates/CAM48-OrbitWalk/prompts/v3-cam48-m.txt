【Goal】
An empty concrete parking structure at night. Alex walks across a bay between red and green fluorescent light, a man who has parked here for a reason he is not telling anyone.

【References】
@video1 is the shot. The camera and every movement of it, the framing, how large Alex is in the frame and where he stands, and the timing all come exactly from @video1 and never from this text. In it the grey set and the striped capsule are only placeholders. The capsule is Alex. Every grey stand-in only marks where something stands: rebuild each as a real, fully detailed object of the same size in the same place, with real materials, textures, wear and dirt; no cone, sphere, plain box or untextured surface appears in the final video, and the grey clay set is not adopted. The capsule's red and black stripes are only a label: no clothing, skin, prop or surface takes those colours or stripes, and no striped capsule appears at any moment. Never show a capsule. @image1 is only for Alex's face, short dark curly hair and beard; ignore its background, lighting and clothes. @image2 is a mood reference only (palette, light, haze, film feel). It is not a layout to copy.

【Place】
Bare grey concrete pillars and a low ceiling, painted bay lines, oil-stained floor, long fluorescent strips spilling crimson on one side and cold green on the other so the floor carries both colours in glossy streaks, a few tiny city lights beyond. Soft bloom on the strips, black shadows with a little colour, fine film grain.

【Alex】
The red-and-black capsule is Alex, who wears a black leather jacket over a dark t-shirt, dark jeans and boots, a set of keys in his right hand.

【Performance】
He walks at an even, deliberate pace with his eyes on something ahead of him, red light and then green sliding across his face and his breath misting in the cold. Once he glances back over his shoulder toward the dark and his pace tightens for a moment, then he closes his hand on the keys so they stop jingling and walks on without looking back.

【Sound and look】
The sound is only his footsteps on concrete, the jingle of keys, the buzz of fluorescent tubes and an echoing drip. No music. Photoreal live-action cinema shot on an ARRI Alexa 35, anamorphic look, soft diffusion and halation on the lights, natural film grain.
