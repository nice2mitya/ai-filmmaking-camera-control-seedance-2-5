One locked-off shot with no cuts and no camera movement at all, on a 16 mm wide-angle lens lying on the boot floor about 10 centimetres up, tilted 49 degrees up toward the tail of the car, at f/11 so everything is in focus.
0 to 0.6 seconds: complete black, with only a hair-thin line of light along the seal.
0.6 to 1.35 seconds: the boot lid swings up 36 degrees in three quarters of a second and daylight floods in; the grey lining of the lid hangs across the top 15 per cent of the frame, a pale city sky and buildings fill the opening below it, and the rubber sill runs along the bottom 4 per cent of the frame.

4.3 to 6.7 seconds: Sam reaches in toward the lens with his right hand, the hand growing large and dark in the lower foreground at about 5.2 seconds, then draws it back out of frame by 6.7 seconds while the other two keep looking down.
From 8 seconds they straighten up away from the sill and their heads rise toward the top of the frame.
8.9 to 9.35 seconds: Alex pushes the lid shut; it swings down in 0.45 seconds, accelerating into the latch with a slam, and the daylight squeezes into a thin slit and vanishes.
