Shot 1, the only shot: the camera is fixed in one spot about three metres from Alex with a 35 mm lens feel, level, framing him from the head to the waist with a large paned window behind him. Over the first four seconds it tilts smoothly down from his eyes, past his chest and hands, to his boots on the bare floor, and it holds on his boots for the last three seconds; the camera never leaves its spot.
Alex's performance: He stands before the window with the crutch under his arm, his gaze fixed straight ahead, fear and pride crossing his face, his jaw set and his breath held. Then he slowly slides the crutch from under his arm and leans it against the sill at his side, his gaze following it as if saying goodbye. He takes one breath, lifts one boot and sets it forward, a single careful step alone, his arms loose, his shoulders easing as it lands. He says nothing.
