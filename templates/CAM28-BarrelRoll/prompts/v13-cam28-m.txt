【Camera and Event Script】
Shot 1, the only shot: the camera is on a rotating gimbal head with a 28 mm lens feel, framing Alex in a medium shot, from the head to the hips, in the middle of the frame. Over the seven seconds it rolls one full 360 degrees about the lens axis, eased at both ends, so the whole room turns upside down at the midpoint and comes back upright at the end, while Alex stays at the centre of the frame, standing upright on the floor, so he is upside down on screen at the midpoint together with the room and upright again at the end.
Alex's performance: He stands in the middle of the bare room with the paper cup, hardly believing it is his. Then he starts to turn slowly on the spot, the cup held against his chest and his free arm half raised, laughing under his breath, his eyes wide and shining and his head tipped back toward the high ceiling. He stops with a long happy exhale, his chest rising and falling and a smile he cannot hold back. He says nothing.
Every action is performed with natural, realistic human motion: real weight and balance, momentum carried through each movement, and loose, believable arms and shoulders. The written description of the action decides how it looks. Alex is a real human being with natural skin, hair and clothing; no mannequin, clay figure, capsule or doll appears anywhere, at any moment.
The sound is only his soft laughter, the faint slosh of wine in a paper cup, the creak of old floorboards, an echo in the empty room and a distant night street. There are no words. There is no music of any kind.
The final visuals are live-action and photographic: photoreal cinema shot on an ARRI Alexa 35 with anamorphic lenses, natural film grain, soft diffusion and halation on the lights, shallow depth of field, motivated light and real atmospheric haze.
