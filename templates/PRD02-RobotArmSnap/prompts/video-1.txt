【Camera and Event Script】
One continuous shot with no cuts, in 2.39:1 widescreen, on a motion-control robot arm. Three stops, each frame already completely still on the beat: no soft landing, no settle, no drift after a stop.
Held wide, from the first frame to about 1.3 seconds: 24 mm lens, 6 metres from the plinth and 2.4 metres up, looking down very slightly. The whole studio is in view, softboxes and flags on both sides, the plinth small in the middle of the frame with the sneaker tiny on top of it. The frame is dead still; only a few dust motes drift in the beams of light.
Move 1, from about 1.3 to 2.0 seconds: the camera crosses the whole studio in seven tenths of a second, a violent, fast move on a descending curve (a distance of about 5.7 metres at a peak of 11 metres a second), the lens tightening from 24 mm to 50 mm as it goes, with strong directional motion blur streaking the softboxes and flags past. At 2.0 seconds it stops dead: an extreme close-up 40 centimetres from the sneaker's outer side, so the frame is filled by the knitted upper and the first lace eyelets, every loop of the weave, every fibre of wool fuzz and the cream laces razor sharp, shallow depth of field, the studio behind dissolved into soft white bokeh. It holds absolutely still until about 3.5 seconds, with only light glinting on the fibres.
Move 2, from about 3.5 to 4.0 seconds: the camera arcs 45 degrees round the plinth and up in half a second (the view turns about 57 degrees), the lens easing to 35 mm, with heavy motion blur. At 4.0 seconds it stops dead on a medium shot 1.25 metres from the sneaker, looking down 39 degrees at it from the front three-quarter side: the whole shoe, the top of the plinth, a softbox glowing behind. It holds absolutely still until about 5.4 seconds.
Move 3, from about 5.4 to 6.0 seconds: the camera drops and swings 66 degrees in six tenths of a second, again with heavy motion blur, down to 3 centimetres above the top of the plinth, 24 mm, 63 centimetres from the sneaker, looking up 11 degrees. At 6.0 seconds it stops dead: a low hero angle, the sneaker towering over the lens in profile with its cream sole and its knit upper against the pale studio, the wide room far behind it. It holds absolutely still for the last two seconds, to the end.
Type of shot: a motion-control launch-film shot, three angles on three beats. Lens and focus: anamorphic, with a slight oval bokeh and a soft horizontal flare on the brightest highlights; depth of field is deep on the wide frame and very shallow on the close-up. Light: one very large soft key from the left softbox wrapping the knit, a hard white kicker from behind the plinth drawing a clean rim along the sole and laces, a soft white bounce filling the shadows, a gradient of light across the white cyclorama; the sneaker casts a soft contact shadow on the plinth.
