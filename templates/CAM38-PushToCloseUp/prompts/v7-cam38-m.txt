【Generation Goal】
Generate a live-action shot in one take. A cramped old shower room flooded with teal light. Alex stands alone under the running water after a long day that has finally caught up with him, and what he feels comes out through his body, not his mouth.

【Subjects and Relationships】
The red-and-black striped capsule in @video1 corresponds to Alex, standing front-on in the middle of the frame, facing the lens. He wears bare shoulders and wet dark hair, water running down his face. The place: Small worn square tiles with dark grout, wet and glossy, a basin with a dripping tap, deep cyan-teal light with navy-black corners and a tiny amber glint at the drain, thin steam near the water, soft bloom on the wet highlights. Claustrophobic and raw, fine film grain.

【Camera and Event Script】
Shot 1, the only shot: the camera is on a slow dolly with an 85 mm lens feel and shallow focus, level with Alex's eyes, facing him front-on. It opens on a medium close-up with his head and shoulders in the middle of the frame and his eyes on the upper third, then pushes straight toward him very slowly over the six seconds, easing in and out, until it arrives on an extreme close-up with his face filling the frame, both eyes still in it. The tiled wall behind him melts further into soft teal as the camera nears. It never pans, tilts or arcs; only the distance closes.
Alex's performance: He starts out braced, his head bowed under the water and his shoulders rigid, his jaw clamped. Then something gives. His breath shudders, his chin trembles and his whole face crumples for a moment before he drags it back, and a small broken sound that is almost a laugh escapes him as his shoulders drop. He pushes his wet hair back, lifts his face into the water with his eyes closed and lets it run over him, breathing slowly until the shaking settles, and when his eyes open they are red and clear. He says nothing.
Every action is performed with natural, realistic human motion: real weight and balance, momentum carried through each movement, and loose, believable arms and shoulders. The written description of the action decides how it looks. Alex is a real human being with natural skin, hair and clothing; no mannequin, clay figure, capsule or doll appears anywhere, at any moment.
The sound is only water running and drumming on tile, his unsteady breathing, one small broken sound in his throat, a tap dripping and a soft echo. There are no words. There is no music of any kind.
The final visuals are live-action and photographic: photoreal cinema shot on an ARRI Alexa 35 with anamorphic lenses, natural film grain, soft diffusion and halation on the lights, shallow depth of field, motivated light and real atmospheric haze.
