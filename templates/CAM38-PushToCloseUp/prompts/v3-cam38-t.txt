【Goal】
A cramped old shower room flooded with teal light. Alex stands alone under the water and says aloud what he has been practising for days.

【Dialogue】
Alex says exactly, low and rough: "I keep rehearsing what I'll say to him, and none of it sounds like me." The film contains this line and no other words.

【Place】
Small worn square tiles with dark grout, wet and glossy, a basin with a dripping tap, deep cyan-teal light with navy-black corners and a tiny amber glint at the drain, thin steam near the water, soft bloom on the wet highlights. Claustrophobic and raw, fine film grain.

【Alex】
The red-and-black capsule is Alex, who wears bare shoulders and wet dark hair, water running down his face.

【Performance】
He has been rehearsing a speech for days and now, tired and alone, the truth comes out: none of it sounds like him. His shoulders rise and fall with each breath and his lips move silently around a rehearsed phrase before he gives up on it. He speaks low and rough, his lips unsteady, his head turning slightly away from his own words, and his voice breaks on "me". Then he stays under the water as it runs down his face, his eyes lowering and slowly lifting, raw and resigned. He says exactly: "I keep rehearsing what I'll say to him, and none of it sounds like me."

【Sound and look】
The sound is only his low voice, a tap dripping, water in far-off pipes, his breathing and a small echo off the tiles. No music. Photoreal live-action cinema shot on an ARRI Alexa 35, anamorphic look, soft diffusion and halation on the lights, natural film grain.
