【Camera and Event Script】
One continuous shot with no cuts. The camera is on a dolly track at standing eye height, about 1.6 metres off the ground and level, with a 35 mm lens feel, the focus held on the statue so the near slab is soft.
Opening: a medium-wide of the statue, a little left of centre, the top of its plinth along the bottom edge, a tall pine directly behind it, columns standing on both sides and round-crowned trees to the right.
The move: the camera slides steadily to the right at a walking pace while panning gently to keep the statue near the centre, so the columns and trees behind shift past it.
The wipe: at about two and a quarter seconds the blurred edge of the stone slab enters from the right edge. It sweeps leftward across the frame, and at about two point eight seconds it fills the entire frame, a soft wall of stone with no statue visible. By about three point two seconds its trailing edge has slid out past the left edge. The move keeps the same speed through the wipe.
The reveal: the statue is there again, now centred, seen from a new angle with different columns and a different pine behind it and a round-crowned tree to the right. The camera keeps sliding and slows gently into the final frame, holding the statue centred.
Mood: anticipation and quiet theatre. The slab is a curtain closing and opening; the statue on the far side feels discovered rather than seen. The light is soft, even daylight, and the pace is smooth and constant so the wipe reads as one clean gesture.
Only the camera moves.
There is no narration, and no subtitles or on-screen text appear.
The sound includes only quiet outdoor ambience. There is no music.
The final visuals are live-action and photographic: a real Mediterranean stone plaza of worn limestone paving, weathered limestone columns, tall Scots pines and leafy plane trees, in crisp late-morning sun. The image is photorealistic and cinematic: a large-format digital cinema camera, natural film grain, shallow depth of field where the lens allows, real atmospheric haze and depth, motivated light with soft falloff and rich but natural contrast, and real textured materials everywhere.
