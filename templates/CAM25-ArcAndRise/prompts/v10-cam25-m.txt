The red-and-black striped capsule in @video1 corresponds to Alex, standing near the middle of the frame, then kneeling at the line where a shop door once stood, seen from a camera that circles him and rises. He wears a long charcoal wool coat with the collar turned up and a grey scarf loose at his throat, his face and beard visible, dust in his hair. 【Camera and Event Script】
Shot 1, the only shot: the camera is on a technocane with a 28 mm lens feel. It starts about chest height and four metres from Alex, facing him on a medium wide shot with him at full length near the middle of the frame and the dusty plaza behind him, then over the eight seconds it arcs about 120 degrees round him while rising about three metres, ending high above and looking down so that he is a small figure in the middle of the frame, the paving and the scattered blocks all around him.
Alex's performance: He has carried the key for years, and now he takes two slow steps to the line in the paving where the shop door once stood and kneels there. He holds the key out in front of him, slides it into empty air at the height of a lock and gives it a quarter turn, with no door, lock or frame appearing, his eyes fixed on nothing and the scarf lifting in the wind. Then his hand drops, his shoulders sink, and he stays kneeling a moment with the key in his fist, breathing slowly in the dust until he straightens. He says nothing.
Every action is performed with natural, realistic human motion: real weight and balance, momentum carried through each movement, and loose, believable arms and shoulders. The written description of the action decides how it looks. Alex is a real human being with natural skin, hair and clothing; no mannequin, clay figure, capsule or doll appears anywhere, at any moment.
The sound is only a low steady wind, grit on fabric and stone, the flap of his coat, the small click of the key and a distant metallic creak. There is no music of any kind.
The final visuals are live-action and photographic: photoreal cinema shot on an ARRI Alexa 35 with anamorphic lenses, natural film grain, soft diffusion and halation on the lights, shallow depth of field, motivated light and real atmospheric haze.
