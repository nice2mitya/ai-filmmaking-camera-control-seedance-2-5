【Camera and Event Script】
One continuous shot with no cuts, on a 28 mm anamorphic lens, 8 seconds.
It opens at about waist-up height, level with his chest, about three metres from him, looking at him from his left front, with the plaza stretching away behind him. From the first frame the camera arcs around him in a smooth, slow, steady curve of about 120 degrees, passing behind his left shoulder and ending on his right rear, and while it circles it rises: by 3 seconds it is above his head height, by 5 seconds it is well above him looking down at him at about thirty degrees, and by 7 seconds it is about three metres higher than where it started, with the whole ruined plaza and the dust-wrapped tower spread around him. Throughout, the camera keeps aiming at his eyes, so his face stays in the upper third of the frame while the world swings behind him.
Alex: he stands with his weight on both feet while the wind pushes at his coat and scarf. At about 2 seconds he lifts one hand and pulls the scarf down from his mouth to his chin, and looks past the lens at the tower with a long, slow breath, jaw set. At about 5 seconds he lowers his eyes, closes them for a moment, and opens them again with a small decided nod. At about 6.5 seconds he takes one slow step forward into the wind. Orange dust streams across the whole frame, thicker in the distance, with gusts that blow grit along the ground and across the lens now and then.
Every action is performed with natural, realistic human motion: real weight and balance, momentum carried through each movement, and loose, believable arms and shoulders. The written description of the action decides how it looks.
Alex is a real human being with natural skin, hair and clothing. No mannequin, clay figure, capsule, faceless figure or doll appears anywhere, at any moment.
Alex keeps his mouth naturally closed and says nothing; there is no narration, and no subtitles or on-screen text appear.
The sound includes only a low steady wind, grit hitting fabric and stone, the flap of his coat and a distant metallic creak. There is no music of any kind: no score, no song, no singing and no humming, only these natural sounds.
The final visuals are live-action and photographic: a real abandoned plaza in burnt-orange atmospheric dust: terracotta and ochre concrete, black silhouettes of buildings, blowing sand on the steps, low sun hidden behind the dust so the light is soft, hazy and orange. The picture is photoreal live-action cinema: shot on an ARRI Alexa 35 with vintage anamorphic prime lenses, 2.39:1, natural 35mm film grain, a soft 1/4 Black Pro-Mist diffusion on the lights, gentle halation round every bright light source, shallow depth of field with oval anamorphic bokeh, real atmospheric haze and depth, motivated practical light with soft falloff, rich but natural contrast with colour kept in the shadows, and real textured materials everywhere. It must look like a still pulled from a feature film, never like a render, a game or a video effect.
