【Camera and Event Script】
Shot 1, the only shot: the camera is on a slow dolly with a 35 mm lens feel, opening on a medium shot with Alex from the head to the waist at the right of the frame and a dark wall behind him. Over the seven seconds it pushes in slowly until it reaches a medium close-up with his head and shoulders large at the right of the frame, while it rolls steadily to a tilt of about 16 degrees, so the walls and the table edge lean more and more as it closes in, and the television stays out of frame.
Alex's performance: He sits close to the television, the blue light flickering over his face, and watches without blinking. As the tiny, tinny laughter on the tape rises, his lips move silently with hers and a smile starts, soft and helpless. Then the smile wavers; his chin trembles, his eyes fill and shine in the glow, and he presses his lips together and swallows, letting the tears sit on his lashes without wiping them. He says nothing.
Every action is performed with natural, realistic human motion: real weight and balance, momentum carried through each movement, and loose, believable arms and shoulders. The written description of the action decides how it looks. Alex is a real human being with natural skin, hair and clothing; no mannequin, clay figure, capsule or doll appears anywhere, at any moment.
The sound is only the tinny, muffled laughter of an old home video with no clear words, the faint electrical hum of the television and his unsteady breathing. There is no music of any kind.
The final visuals are live-action and photographic: photoreal cinema shot on an ARRI Alexa 35 with anamorphic lenses, natural film grain, soft diffusion and halation on the lights, shallow depth of field, motivated light and real atmospheric haze.
