One continuous shot with no cuts, from a drone on a gimbal with a 35 mm lens feel and deep focus, in three stages.
Stage 1, dive, 0 to about 5.6 seconds: it opens about thirty metres up and far behind the car, looking steeply down; the car is small, about a tenth of the frame width, on the road curving up the frame between trees. Already flying at the car's speed, the drone dives smoothly down and forward without any jolt, a tall pine sweeping past close on the right at about 2 seconds, the gimbal tilting up as it descends, until it rides about two metres above the road just behind the car.
Stage 2, chase, about 5.6 to 8.6 seconds: it holds there at the car's speed, the car large and centred from behind and slightly above, the road curving away ahead, poles flicking past on the right and the lane dashes streaming under the lens.
Stage 3, rise, about 8.6 seconds to the end: the drone climbs and falls back in one eased move, the car shrinking on the winding road as the hills and trees spread out around it, ending high and wide.
