【Camera and Event Script】
One continuous shot with no cuts, in slow motion throughout, about a third of real speed. The camera is very low, about knee height, tilted up about twenty to twenty-five degrees on a long lens of about 85 mm, so the two men's heads and shoulders tower against the sky and no ground is ever in frame. It circles them at a steady, unhurried rate of about seventeen degrees a second, never stopping and never shaking, travelling to its own right so the background wheels across the frame behind them.
0 to 1.6 seconds: the camera is behind Alex's right shoulder. We see the back of his head and shoulders as he rises slowly from a crouch to his full height, palm trees and a far building sliding past beyond him.
1.3 to 3 seconds: as the camera comes round his right side, Sam rises into the frame from below, beside and just beyond Alex's shoulder.
3 to 4.5 seconds: both men are in profile, standing tall, chins lifted, eyes fixed on something far off; a long white building front and its roofline stream past behind them with a strip of blue sky above.
4.5 seconds to the end: the camera comes round to their front. Both men turn their heads slowly to look off past the lens, jaws set, breathing hard through the nose, Sam higher and larger on the right of the frame and Alex lower on the left, open sky and a low building behind them.
Their movement is slow and heavy with weight: shoulders squaring, chests rising with each breath, sweat running down their temples, shirts shifting in a light wind. Sunlight flares into the lens as it passes behind their heads.
Every action is performed with natural, realistic human motion: real weight and balance, momentum carried through each movement, and loose, believable arms and shoulders. The written description of the action decides how it looks.
Every person in the video is a real human being with natural skin, hair and clothing. No mannequin, clay figure, capsule, faceless figure or doll appears anywhere, at any moment.
Characters keep their mouths naturally closed; there is no narration, and no subtitles or on-screen text appear.
The sound includes only a low wind, distant sirens and slow heavy breathing, all stretched by the slow motion. There is no music.
The final visuals are live-action and photographic: a real sun-bleached coastal city street: white and pastel Art Deco buildings, tall palm trees, street lamps and a deep blue sky with scattered white clouds, in hard midday sun with strong highlights, warm skin tones, lens flares and heat haze. The image is photorealistic and cinematic: a large-format digital cinema camera, natural film grain, shallow depth of field where the lens allows, real atmospheric haze and depth, motivated light with soft falloff and rich but natural contrast, and real textured materials everywhere.
