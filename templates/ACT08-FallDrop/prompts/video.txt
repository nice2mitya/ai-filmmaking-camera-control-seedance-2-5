【Camera and Event Script】
One continuous shot with no cuts, on a technocrane.
0 to about 1.5 seconds: the camera holds at standing eye height just behind Sam's right shoulder with a 35 mm lens feel, Sam's back large on the right, Alex past him in a medium shot from the thighs up. Alex raises both fists into guard, eyes fixed on Sam.
About 1.5 to 2.2 seconds: Alex pivots on the back foot and drives a straight cross into Sam's chest. Sam's torso jolts and he stumbles back toward the camera, knees giving.
About 2.2 to 3.75 seconds: Sam's legs fold and he topples backward toward the camera side onto the paving. A beat after he starts to fall, never before, the camera drops straight down in under a second, accelerating like a falling weight, widening slightly to a 28 mm feel, its aim riding Sam's head down a fraction behind, and stops dead just above the floor about a body's length from Sam's head.
About 3.75 seconds to the end: Sam lies on his back across the foreground, head and shoulders nearest the lens on the left, body and arms stretching away to the right. Alex stands beyond, full length, centred left, towering against the treetops in a low-angle wide, still in guard. From about 4.6 to 5.6 seconds the focus pulls smoothly from Sam's head to Alex, Sam softening into a blurred foreground shape, and Alex slowly lowers both fists to the sides. The frame holds still to the end.
