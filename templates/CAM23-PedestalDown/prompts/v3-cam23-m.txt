【Goal】
A late-afternoon café full of hazy sun. Alex sits across a small table from someone he has not seen in years and tells them, with a smile he cannot hold back, that they have not changed.

【Dialogue】
Alex says exactly, warmly, a little amazed: "You look exactly the way I remembered." The film contains this line and no other words.

【References】

In it the grey set and the striped capsule are only placeholders. The capsule is Alex. 【Place】
Honey and golden cream, brown wood, tall windows with low sun coming through haze and steam in soft broad beams, hanging plants, a milky glow with soft bloom on every bright edge, warm shadows. Dreamy and analog, light diffusion, fine film grain.

【Alex】
The red-and-black capsule is Alex, who wears a worn tan corduroy jacket over a grey t-shirt.

【Performance】
He came ready to be casual about it, and seeing them across the table takes that away. Before he can stop it the smile arrives and his eyes crinkle. He speaks straight to them, a little amazed at his own warmth, and the last word goes soft and tender with a slight catch. Then he lets out a small laugh at himself and keeps looking at them, still smiling, as if checking they are really there. He says exactly: "You look exactly the way I remembered."

【Sound and look】
The sound is only his warm voice, the murmur of a nearly empty café, cups on saucers and the hiss of a coffee machine. No music. Photoreal live-action cinema shot on an ARRI Alexa 35, anamorphic look, soft diffusion and halation on the lights, natural film grain.
