【Camera and Event Script】
Fly-through: one continuous shot from a small cinewhoop FPV drone with a 24 mm wide-lens feel and deep focus, the horizon held level throughout.
It opens outside at about two and a half metres up, a little left of the window, facing the facade square on from across the open ground. The pale facade fills the frame; the window is just below and right of centre, small, with the statue visible through it on its plinth, and the blind panels sit evenly to either side.
The drone flies straight at the window in one smooth line, descending slightly. For the first two and a half seconds the window grows only slowly; then, as the drone gathers speed, it swells fast. At about three seconds the window opening fills the frame and its frame edges rush past within a metre of the lens at the fastest point, flicking by on all four sides. The drone does not slow in the opening.
By three and a half seconds it is inside: the gallery opens up, skylight pouring down, a hard diagonal shadow on the left wall, the statue on its plinth in the centre of the frame, small and full-length, the back wall and pilasters behind. The drone keeps flying forward across the floor, slowing smoothly, the statue growing until it fills about three quarters of the frame height. From about six seconds it eases to a hover and holds a front-on medium shot: the statue's head in the upper third, its raised arm reaching toward the upper right, the plinth along the bottom, the pilasters framing it behind.
Only the camera moves.
