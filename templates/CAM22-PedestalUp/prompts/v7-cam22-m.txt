The red-and-black striped capsule in @video1 corresponds to Alex, standing front-on in the centre of the frame, facing the lens. 【Camera and Event Script】
Shot 1, the only shot: the camera is on a pedestal with a 50 mm lens feel, facing Alex front-on at a steady distance, in a medium close-up with Alex's head and shoulders in the middle of the frame, with a little space above his head. It starts about 0.6 metres below his eye line looking slightly up, and over the six seconds it rises smoothly and steadily to about 0.9 metres above his eye line looking slightly down, with no change in distance: Alex stays the same size in the middle of the frame while the corridor behind him slides downward past him. It never pans or zooms.
Alex's performance: He holds a paper cup against his chest so tightly that his knuckles have gone pale, his eyes fixed on the door and his jaw set. Then a muffled murmur and a soft, relieved laugh come from behind it and his eyes snap up. His breath stops, his brows pull together and his jaw loosens as the meaning reaches him. Tears fill his eyes and he blinks them back, lets out a long shaky breath, presses his lips together and gives one small, disbelieving nod while his chest sinks with relief. He says nothing.
Every action is performed with natural, realistic human motion: real weight and balance, momentum carried through each movement, and loose, believable arms and shoulders. The written description of the action decides how it looks. Alex is a real human being with natural skin, hair and clothing; no mannequin, clay figure, capsule or doll appears anywhere, at any moment.
The sound is only the hum of fluorescent tubes, a muffled murmur and a soft relieved laugh from behind the door with no clear words, a trolley wheel far away and his unsteady breathing. There is no music of any kind.
The final visuals are live-action and photographic: photoreal cinema shot on an ARRI Alexa 35 with anamorphic lenses, natural film grain, soft diffusion and halation on the lights, shallow depth of field, motivated light and real atmospheric haze.
