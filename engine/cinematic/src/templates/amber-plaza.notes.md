# AMBER plaza take (0 to 25.04 s): notes

File: `amber-plaza.ts`. Exports: `plazaSet`, `PLAZA_MAN_PATH`, `PLAZA_MAN_ACTS`, `plazaShots` (and `CAM_KEYS`). One shot, one continuous camera path, no cut.
World frame as in `amber-world.ts`: wall face is the plane z = 0, plaza is z < 0, the man walks +z, kneels at KNEEL (0, -0.95).
Lens: 39 mm on the dive and the close-up (0 to 9.5 s), easing to 55 mm by 12.5 s, 55 mm to the end. The camera stands nearer on the short lens so the man keeps his size.

## 1. What the take is, in the world I built

The two analysts used two different worlds. I fitted both to the frames and to one rigid wall:

- The man walks toward the camera along a line that runs toward -x and +z. He starts at (1.3, -5.8) and stops at (-2.87, -3.78) at 4.8 s.
- The camera starts at (-39.7, 10.9, +14.2), behind the left end of the wall, 46 m from him and 11 m up, and flies down his line. It crosses the wall plane at about x = -10.6, 3.8 s, at about 2 m height. The wall face is a one sided plane (it faces -z), so the camera passes through it without seeing it.
- He braces 4.9 to 9.8 s, takes two heavy steps toward the wall 10.0 to 11.1 s, and stands in a sand ring at (-1.95, -3.7) until 15.25 s.
- The camera is 3.6 m from the wall from 14 to 18 s, on his right (the -x side), looking along the wall (+x) with the wall on its right. This is why the brick wall fills the right of the frame at 14.5 to 18 s and recedes to the left. The shutter comes in at the right edge at 17.9 s.
- From 18.5 s the camera swings round behind him and backs away to 10.7 m from the wall (21 s), then dollies in to 7.6 m (23.9 s) and locks.
- The man walks slowly toward the wall (15.4 to 18.2 s), turns left along the wall (18 to 21 s), turns right to the shutter at 21 s, lunges and kneels 24.2 to 25.0 s.

The tower is a plain tapered block 420 m away on the bearing 106 degrees (x 400, z -125). A far bunker ramp sits at (100, -14).

## 4. What the join and the shop builder must know

1. The wall face is single sided (it faces -z) so the dive camera, which starts behind the left end of the wall, never sees its back. The face runs from x = -20 to x = +60 (longer than the contract's +20, because the camera sees it receding for 40 m at 15 to 18 s). It has an opening at the shutter bay (x -1.7 to +1.7, y 0 to 2.6): the face, the brick lines and the plinth stop at the edges. The shop builder owns the trim, the shutter and the closed back of the wall.
2. Pilasters (a stone foot 1.0 x 1.1 m and a steel strap 0.3 m wide) stand at x = +-(4.4 + 4.2 k). The plinth is 0.9 m high, 0.35 m deep, along the foot of the wall except across the bay and the two posts. The posts are fitted at x = 1.7 to 2.05 either side (outer edge 2.05).
3. The dive camera is behind the wall at 0 to 3.7 s and looks toward -z at the man, with the cone about 50 degrees wide. The shop (z 0 to 5.5, x -2.6 to 2.6) lies inside that cone at the right of the man (bearing about 10 degrees right of the man's line). Hide it for t < 4 s (or keep it low and far), or it shows in the aerial frames. The original shows nothing there.
4. Scenery tagged with `dress()`: slab joints, the three damp patches, the drifts, the stones, the far ramp and block. A `--no-scenery` render removes them. The ground, the wall, the plinth, the pilasters and the tower stay.
5. The moving layer: the join's SceneSpec must supply `motes` (few, small, white, drifting -x at 0.25 m/s). My test piece used 60 motes in a 40 x 6 x 40 m box centred (0, 2.5, -8). The plaza set adds no sand, dust or cloud geometry.
6. The rig label is `STEADICAM · GIMBAL` (float 6 mm, 0.06 degrees), so the locked part drifts under 2 cm/s. Do not put "DRONE" in the label (it adds a 5 cm/s float).
7. Sky: I tested with `mono`, light from (-0.4, 0.55, -0.75). The wall is in shadow if the key comes from behind it.
8. Camera speeds: dive peak 23 m/s (average 8.5 m/s, 0 to 4.75 s), 0.1 m/s while he braces (4.75 to 9.5), 0.7 m/s average to 18 s, 2.8 m/s average (peak 5.8) in the swing 18 to 21 s, 1.4 m/s in the dolly 21 to 23.9 s, under 0.02 m/s locked.

## 7. What does not match, and why

1. **Bent posture is a crouch.** The pin has one crouch height (eye 0.95 m), and the original man is bent 30 to 50 degrees with his head at about 1.3 m. From 12.9 to 18.2 s the pin is lower than the man; I put the camera 0.3 to 0.4 m lower (0.8 to 0.9 m high, the original is 1.0 to 1.2 m) to put the head in the right place. From 18.7 to 22.5 s the man is still bent 30 degrees but the pin stands tall.
2. **Crouch walk slides.** `Crouch_Fwd_Loop` is time based, not tied to the distance walked. At 0.5 to 0.8 m/s the feet slip 0.4 to 0.8 of the body speed. The pin has no feet and the frame shows only the torso at 15.5 to 18.2 s, but the number is there. Walking upright with the foot-locked gait would have lost the bent pose; I kept the pose and the pace the clip likes. At 10.2 to 10.3 s the first step and at 20.8 to 21.4 s the turn at the shutter pass through 0.55 to 0.9 m/s, where the stepping blends; at 24.4 s the kneel starts while he still moves at 0.8 m/s.
3. **SAFE fails at 12.5 s** (the man's middle is above the frame) and **JOINT** (knees and ankles cut by the bottom edge): the original is a legs-only frame from 10.5 to 13.5 s. Deliberate. There is no flag for either.
4. **PAN 32 degrees a second at 20.3 s.** The original swing round behind him from 18.5 to 21 s is fast and its geometry needs the camera to move 5 m in a second while it turns 30 degrees. I kept it. The man stays near the middle of the frame through it (0.54, 0.49).
5. **HOLD fails (moves in the first 0.22 s).** The dive is at its fastest in the first second of the original. Deliberate.
6. **Camera speeds are not natural for a drone** in the dive (peak 23 m/s). They follow the original's frames (the man grows from 0.10 to 0.53 of the frame height in 3 s). The lens (39 mm) was chosen from the first analyst's 50 degree field of view; with 55 mm the peak was 33 m/s.
7. **The wall is a long plane** from x = -20 to +60, not -20 to +20, and the man's walk is parallel to it at 3 to 4 m. The two analysts' wall distances (3.6 m in 14 to 18 s, 10 m from 19 s) come out right with this camera path, but the first analyst's man (11 m from the wall) and the second's x positions do not. I used the frames, not their numbers.
8. **The tower and the ramp are not rigid.** In the original the tower leaves the left edge at 13 s and comes back at 14 s, and a ramp at the right at 12.5 s is at the far left at 15 s. One far tower (x 400, z -125) and one far ramp (100, -14) cannot do both. The tower is at the right at 0 to 9.5 s (as in the original, 0.77 of the frame), at the left from 12 s; the ramp is at the right of the frame at 12.5 s and drifts left. The tower is a plain tapered block. The second annex is a single block, not the stepped one.
9. **Brick spacing:** course lines every 0.15 m (two courses), not the true 0.075 m, because finer lines shimmer at the render sizes. No vertical joints.
10. **Sand:** the real drifts are 0.25 to 0.6 m high mounds along the foot of the wall and in a ring round the boots. Mine are low grey half-spheres at the positions the frames show (a cone of 0.62 m against the +x post, a low spread against the -x post). They show more than in the original from the aerial side at 0 to 3 s (the wall's foot is in the cone of the dive).
11. **No effects:** the heavy dust, the sand skating, the haze and the scarf and coat blowing are in the script, not in the set. The frame is far clearer than the original at 0 to 14.5 s. The fog is the join's sky.
12. **Hands, face and mouth** are in the script. The pin has no mouth at the two "Oh" (9.84 to 11.66 s); the camera is on his legs from 10.5 s anyway.
13. **The original frame is kept in `build/plaza/orig/` (640 px, 1 s steps).** Heights of the camera (0.8 to 1.0 m at 12.5 to 18 s) are the least certain numbers in the take, as the second analyst said.
