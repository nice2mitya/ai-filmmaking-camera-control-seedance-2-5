File: `engine/cinematic/src/templates/amber-shop.ts`. Test piece used for the checks (in my scratch copy only): `amber-test.ts`, id `AMB01-ShopTest`.
World frame, units and clock are those of `amber-world.ts` (metres, 24 fps, one clock). Facing the wall (+z), +x is the man's LEFT. Wall face at z = 0, shop at z > 0.

## 0. What the join must know

1. Exports with the contract names: `shopSet`, `shopActors`, `SHOP_MAN_PATH`, `SHOP_MAN_ACTS`, `shopShots`, `MAN_KNEEL_T1`. Extras: `shopExposure(t)`, `SHOP_ROOM`, `shopWallAroundOpening(w)`, `CAMS`.
2. `MAN_KNEEL_T1 = 25.84`. The kit's `kneel` act is a clip of 5.2 s that kneels, fixes something and STANDS UP again (the engine loops it). It cannot hold an 11 s kneel. From 25.04 the shop part holds the kneel with a `crouch` act (a steady clip, head 0.82 m, bent over the lock) that fades in over 0.8 s while the plaza's `kneel` act fades out. The join sets the plaza kneel act's `t1 = MAN_KNEEL_T1`. `amber-plaza.ts` as delivered builds that act with `t0 24.2` and `ease 0.8`, so it keeps full weight to 25.04 (25.84 - 0.8) and is in the flat part of its loop (0.9 to 3.9 s after t0, plus the engine's 0.4 s phase offset) from 24.2 to 26.7. Keep `ease 0.8` and `t0` between 22.0 and 24.4 or change `MAN_KNEEL_T1` and the `ease` of my `crouch` act (`SHOP_MAN_ACTS[0]`) with it. Checked in a join test (`AMB02-JoinTest`: the plaza part as delivered plus this file): the last plaza frame (24.96, kneeling pin) and the first shop frame (25.04, the pin's head at the same height) agree.
3. The plaza wall has the opening (x -1.7 to +1.7, y 0 to 2.6) in `amber-plaza.ts` as delivered, so `shopWallAroundOpening` is not needed (it stays in the file as a fallback). The trim of the opening (jambs and lintel 0.3 m deep behind the wall face) is part of the prop ROOM.

ROOM is buried 40 m deep until 4.0 s, sinks no higher than 3 m below the ground from 20 s and rises to its place between 24.0 and 24.8 s, behind the wall and the closed shutter, where the plaza camera cannot see it.
5. `shopActors` = ROOM, MOTHER, SHUTTER, KEY, SCARF, COAT, COATHEAP, CUP. All paths are on the shared clock. SHUTTER is present from t = 0, closed until 29.9 s, then `shutterLift` sampled every 0.05 s. KEY, SCARF, COAT, CUP and COATHEAP are buried at y = -5 while they are not in play. 8. Fade to black: the film ends in black with the bulb dimming from 55.0 s. ## 2. Decisions and assumptions

**(a) The cut at 47.33 hides a time jump.** Assumed: about 6 s of story pass inside the cut. In that time the mother fetches the tea from the far end of the table (where the till and the tins are), pours it and sets the cup in front of the chair; the man crosses the 0.5 m to the chair and sits; the coat and the scarf (a pile under the coat) are pushed along the table by the mother as she makes room for the cup; the key goes into his overall pocket.
 What is the same on both sides of the cut: his head is bowed, his arms hang or lie on the table, the mother's green cardigan, the warm light, the scarf and the coat are on the table. Before the cut (47.29): he stands at (0.1, 0.62), facing -x, head down about 20 degrees, key in his right hand, mother bent over the table's near-left corner laying the coat. After the cut (47.33): he sits at (0.55, 2.2) facing -z, head down, forearms on the table, the cup 0.1 m left of his chest centre, the mother standing at (1.45, 0.86), her left hand on the table beside the cup (not visible: the pin has no arms). Both bodies jump at 47.32 (see section 0, point 10). The coat heap moves from (0.7, 1.4) to (1.85, 1.45) and the scarf under it with it; the original also shows the pile about 0.8 m further along in shot 6.

**(b) The man in shot 6 is 1.5 to 2 times too big for his place in the room** (segD). I tried both fixes against frame 53.21. Scaling him is not possible for one shot only (the kit scales an actor for the whole film). So I moved him: he sits at x = 0.55, near the table's near end and its right-hand corner, which is where the man is in the original frame (his shoulders overlap the near-right corner of the table at px 1120). His head is 0.2 m wide. With the 25 mm camera 2.4 m away it is 71 px of 1280, against 115 px in the original (-38 percent): the pin is slimmer than the actor in the film and nothing can be done in this shot without breaking the room. His position in the frame (head 70 percent across, original 73 percent) and the pose (leaning forward on the table, head down) match. The cup stays in front of him, 0.1 m from his chest, in both shots (in the original it is at the far end by the tins in shot 6 and in front of his chest in shot 5: it cannot be both and a cup that jumps at a cut breaks the user's rule 1). The man and the cup are therefore 1.5 m from the tins in my shot 6, 2.0 m in the original frame.

**(c) The mother is not ducking under the bar** (she is already in the doorway in the first frame of shot 3). She walks to the doorway behind the closed shutter from 29.5 and takes the bar at 30.4 to 31.1; her path is hidden by the shutter and by shot 2's frame. Her eye is 1.48 m up, her top 1.6 m, so she is under the bar from 32.6 s (bar 1.75 m) and just touching it at 32.17 (bar 1.6 m), as in the original (3 to 4 cm under).

**(d) The man's turn at the end of shot 3.** In the original he faces away from the camera (north) in the last frame of shot 3 and faces the camera in the first frame of shot 4. His head is hidden behind the bar in the last frames of shot 3, so I turn him from 40.5 s on (the pin's body is round; only the face dot shows it), heading -x by 41.21.

**(e) Shot 3 to shot 4, positions.** The film is not consistent here (the mother at x about 0.3 in shot 3, about 1.3 in shot 4). I put her at x 0.75, z 0.3 at 41.21 (hand on the bar at the far side of the doorway), the man at (0.1, 0.6). At the last frame of shot 3 she is at the left edge of the frame as in the original.

**(f) The cup goes down before the cut at 53.21.** In the original the last frame of shot 5 has the cup in both hands under his chin and the first frame of shot 6 has it on the table, both hands round it. To keep the cut continuous I lower the cup to the table from 52.4 to 52.9 (his hands stay round it: not visible), 0.3 s before the cut. In shot 5 the cup leaves the bottom of the frame as he sets it down. This is the one place where the action of the original is changed.

**(g) Heights.** The kit's rig has no kneel-upright pose. Kneel (head 0.81 m, 0.56 m ahead of the knees) is held by the `crouch` clip (head 0.82 m); "kneeling upright, head 1.3 m" (32.17 to 36) is the `sit` clip (head 1.33 m). The stoop under the bar (38.4 to 39.5) is a bump of the `crouch` clip with weight 0.25 (head 1.62 to 1.5 m).

**(h) The pin sits over the middle of the body, not over the head** (`standins.ts`: the pin's x and z are the middle of the joints' box). For a man who leans over a lock the pin head therefore sits 0.3 m behind the real head; the camera of shot 1 and the scarf and coat props follow the pin (their paths are baked from the real pin positions every 0.25 s from 25.0 to 41.0).
